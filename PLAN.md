# How mobile phones work — motion plan

**Idea:** Follow a message from a phone, through radio waves and a cell tower, to another phone.

**Format:** 24-second, 16:9 motion explainer with a seekable browser preview. No external voice or music was provided, so the story is told through on-screen copy.

**Look:** Warm editorial paper, ink-black typography, and electric violet. Soft, dimensional product lighting and simple geometric forms keep the technology approachable.

| Time | Story | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6s | Your phone turns your voice into digital data. | Phone eases into view; data particles pulse. |
| 6–12s | It sends that data as radio waves. | Violet wave rings radiate from the phone. |
| 12–18s | A nearby cell tower passes it into the network. | Tower rises into view and signals travel outward. |
| 18–24s | The network routes it to another phone. | Two phones connect; the message arrives. |

**Camera:** One perspective lens with a gentle eased orbit. Every scene pose is a deterministic function of time, so the timeline can be scrubbed in either direction.

**Preview:** Interactive playback, pause, chapter selection and timeline scrubbing. The original kit remains in `film-kit/` for offline rendering workflows; this preview uses a lightweight real-time renderer so it can play directly in the browser.
