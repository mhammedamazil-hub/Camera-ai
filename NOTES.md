# Notes

## Version 1
- Built a 24-second, four-chapter explainer with a procedural Three.js phone, radio rings, cell tower and signal particles.
- Used deterministic time-based animation, eased camera motion, beveled geometry and soft key/fill lighting inspired by the supplied kit.
- No narration or music was supplied; the browser preview is intentionally silent.

## Version 2 — MP4 with sound
- Rendered the interactive film frame-by-frame at 1280 × 720, 30 fps, 720 frames.
- Composed a procedural stereo electronic bed, radio chirps and transition sweeps; mixed and encoded AAC with FFmpeg loudness normalization. No narration.
- Encoded H.264 MP4: 24.00 seconds, measured -14.3 LUFS integrated, -1.6 dBTP.
